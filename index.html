PRODUCTION TIPS & BEST PRACTICES


📱 REEL PRODUCTION GUIDELINES

  • Shoot in 9:16 vertical format (1080×1920px) for Instagram/YouTube Shorts
  • Keep subtitles ON — 85% of reels are watched without sound
  • Use jump cuts every 2-3 seconds to maintain energy
  • Record in good natural light or ring light — avoid shadows
  • Add trending background music at 20-30% volume
  • Always include your logo watermark in the bottom corner
  • Hook must appear in the FIRST 2 seconds or viewers scroll away
  • Film 3 takes of every scene — use the most natural one

📱 CAROUSEL DESIGN GUIDELINES

  • Design in 1:1 square (1080×1080px) or 4:5 portrait for max reach
  • Slide 1 MUST stop the scroll — bold headline, strong visual
  • Keep each slide to ONE idea — do not overwhelm
  • Use consistent brand colors: CredFix Navy #0A1628, Gold #F59E0B, Blue #2563EB
  • End every carousel with a clear CTA slide

🖼 STATIC POST GUIDELINES

  • Size: 1080×1080px square or 1080×1350px portrait
  • Strong visual hierarchy: Big headline → Supporting text → CTA
  • Post at peak hours: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-10 PM IST
  • Save-worthy content performs best — give real value
